# Piano Tiles

A fast-paced rhythm game where players tap falling black tiles before they reach the bottom of the screen.

## How to Play

1. Tap the "Start Game" button to begin
2. Black tiles will start falling from the top in one of four columns
3. Tap each black tile before it reaches the bottom
4. Don't tap the white areas between tiles
5. The game speeds up as your score increases
6. Miss a tile or tap a white area = Game Over!

## Game Features

- **Progressive Difficulty**: Speed increases every 10 tiles tapped
- **Score Tracking**: Keep track of your highest scores
- **Clean Design**: Simple black and white tiles with smooth animations
- **Touch Optimized**: Responsive touch controls perfect for mobile play
- **Instant Feedback**: Visual effects when tiles are tapped

## Game Mechanics

- Tiles fall continuously from the top
- Speed starts at 1x and increases by 0.1x every 10 successful taps
- Each successful tap earns 1 point
- Game ends when you miss a tile or tap a white area

## Technical Details

- **Technologies**: HTML5, CSS3, Vanilla JavaScript
- **Mobile Optimized**: Touch-friendly with responsive design
- **Browser Requirements**: Modern browsers (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge)
- **No Dependencies**: Pure web technologies, no external libraries

## Tips for High Scores

- Stay focused on the falling tiles
- Use quick, precise taps
- Don't rush - accuracy is more important than speed
- Practice to get better at higher speeds
