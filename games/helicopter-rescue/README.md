# Helicopter Rescue 🚁

Pilot a helicopter and complete daring rescue missions to save people and animals!

## How to Play

1. Tap **Start Mission** to begin
2. Use the **◄ ►** arrows to move your helicopter left and right
3. Tap the **🪝 Hook** button to lower and raise the rescue hook
4. Position the helicopter above people/animals on the ground
5. Lower the hook to pick them up and rescue them!
6. Rescue everyone before running out of fuel

## Rescue Targets

Save different people and animals for points:

- 🧑 👨 👩 **People** (10 points each)
- 👦 👧 **Children** (15 points each)
- 🐕 **Dog** (20 points)
- 🐈 **Cat** (20 points)
- 🎒 **Supplies** (5 points)

## Mission Ranks

Earn ranks based on rescues completed:

- **Hero Pilot** 🌟: 8 rescues (Perfect!)
- **Expert Rescuer** 🏅: 6-7 rescues
- **Skilled Pilot** ⭐: 4-5 rescues
- **Brave Rescuer** 🎖️: 2-3 rescues
- **Novice Pilot** 🔰: 0-1 rescues

## Tips

- Watch your fuel gauge carefully
- Position the helicopter precisely above targets
- Deploy the hook at the right moment
- Try to rescue everyone for a perfect mission!

## Features

- Realistic helicopter physics with hovering animation
- Dynamic hook deployment system
- Multiple rescue targets with different values

- **Mobile**: Use on-screen buttons (◄ ► for movement, 🪝 for hook)
