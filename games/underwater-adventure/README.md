# 🌊 Underwater Adventure

Dive deep into the ocean, collect treasures, and manage your oxygen supply!

## How to Play

1. **Tap to Swim**: Click or tap anywhere on the screen to swim to that location
2. **Collect Treasures**: Gather valuable items for points
3. **Watch Your Oxygen**: It depletes 0.5% per second
4. **Grab Air Bubbles**: Restore 20% oxygen when collected
5. **Avoid Jellyfish**: They drain 30% oxygen on contact
6. **Survive**: Don't let your oxygen reach zero!

## Collectibles

### Treasures (Points)
- **💎 Diamond** - 50 points (rare)
- **🦪 Pearl** - 30 points (uncommon)
- **🪙 Coin** - 10 points (common)

### Oxygen Management
- **🫧 Air Bubble** - Restores 20% oxygen
- **☠️ Jellyfish** - Drains 30% oxygen (danger!)

## Features

- **Tap-to-Swim Controls**: Intuitive one-tap navigation
- **Oxygen System**: Constantly depleting resource adds urgency
- **Depth Tracking**: See how deep you've explored
- **Visual Oxygen Bar**: Color-coded warning system
- **Animated Ocean**: Background bubbles and floating items
- **Score Tracking**: Points and maximum depth reached

## Game Mechanics

- **Oxygen Depletion**: Loses 0.5% per second automatically
- **Movement**: Diver swims to tapped location with smooth animation
- **Depth Calculation**: Based on vertical position in ocean
- **Spawn System**: Items appear randomly every 1.2 seconds
- **Collision Detection**: Automatic collection on contact

## Oxygen Bar States

- **Green** (100-51%): Safe oxygen levels
- **Orange** (50-31%): Low oxygen warning
- **Red** (30-0%): Critical - flashing warning!

## Item Spawn Rates

- Coins: 30%
- Pearls: 20%
- Treasures: 15%
- Air Bubbles: 20%
- Jellyfish: 15%

## Strategy Tips

- Prioritize air bubbles when oxygen is below 50%
- Avoid jellyfish at all costs - they're very costly
- Diamonds are worth 5x coins - worth the risk!
- Stay in the middle area for easier item access
- Watch the oxygen bar color for warnings
