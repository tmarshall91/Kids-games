# 🐠 Aquarium Builder

Build your own beautiful aquarium by collecting and arranging colorful sea creatures!

## How to Play

1. **Watch for Fish**: A new fish appears in the spawner area every few seconds
2. **Catch the Fish**: Tap the fish to catch it and add it to your tank
3. **Arrange Your Tank**: Drag fish around to arrange them however you like
4. **Collect Them All**: Try to collect all 10 different types of sea creatures
5. **Build Your Score**: Different creatures give different points

## Features

- **10 Sea Creatures**: Fish 🐟🐠🐡, sharks 🦈, octopus 🐙, squid 🦑, shrimp 🦐, crab 🦀, shell 🐚, and starfish ⭐
- **Drag & Drop**: Arrange your fish anywhere in the tank
- **Animated Swimming**: Fish swim with realistic motion
- **Bubble Effects**: Relaxing bubble animations
- **Score System**: Earn points for each creature caught
- **Tank Limit**: Maximum 15 creatures in your tank

## Sea Creature Rarity

- **Common** (10 points): 🐟 🐠
- **Rare** (20 points): 🐡 🦈 🦐 🦀
- **Special** (30 points): 🐙 🦑 🐚 ⭐

## Game Mechanics

- New fish spawn every 2 seconds in the catch area
- Each fish spawns randomly from all 10 types
- Drag fish to rearrange them in your tank
- Clear tank button to start fresh (requires confirmation)
- Unlimited playtime - build your perfect aquarium!
