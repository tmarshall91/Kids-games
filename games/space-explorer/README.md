# 🚀 Space Explorer

Navigate through dangerous asteroid fields, collect stars, and explore the cosmos!

## How to Play

1. **Control Your Spaceship**: Swipe or drag across the screen to move
2. **Collect Stars**: ⭐ stars give +10 points
3. **Avoid Asteroids**: ☄️ asteroids cause -1 life
4. **Grab Planets**: 🪐 planets give +25 bonus points
5. **Survive**: Don't lose all 3 lives!
6. **Progressive Difficulty**: Game gets faster the longer you survive

## Features

- **Drag Controls**: Smooth spaceship movement following your finger/mouse
- **Three Object Types**: Stars, asteroids, and bonus planets
- **Progressive Difficulty**: Speed increases every 10 seconds
- **Lives System**: 3 lives to start, lose one per asteroid hit
- **Score Tracking**: Points and survival time
- **Collision Effects**: Visual feedback for all interactions
- **Starfield Background**: Animated space backdrop

## Game Objects

- **⭐ Stars** (50% spawn rate)
  - Collectible
  - Worth 10 points
  - Common, frequent spawns

- **☄️ Asteroids** (35% spawn rate)
  - Dangerous
  - Costs 1 life on collision
  - Must be avoided

- **🪐 Planets** (15% spawn rate)
  - Rare collectible
  - Worth 25 points
  - Bonus high-value items

## Difficulty Progression

Every 10 seconds:
- Spawn rate increases (objects appear more frequently)
- Fall speed increases (objects move faster)
- Maximum difficulty reached after ~60 seconds

## Scoring System

- **Stars**: +10 points each
- **Planets**: +25 points each
- **Lives**: Start with 3, lose 1 per asteroid hit
- **Game Over**: When all lives are lost

## Controls

- **Touch**: Drag your finger across the screen
- **Mouse**: Click and drag to move the spaceship
- **Movement**: Spaceship follows your touch/cursor position

## Technical Details

- **Technologies**: HTML5, CSS3, Vanilla JavaScript
- **Mobile-First**: Fully responsive and touch-optimized
- **No Dependencies**: Runs entirely in the browser
- **Browser Support**: iOS Safari 12+, Chrome 80+, Firefox 75+, Edge 80+
- **Animation**: requestAnimationFrame for smooth gameplay

## Educational Value

- **Hand-Eye Coordination**: Track and avoid moving objects
- **Reaction Speed**: Quick decisions under pressure
- **Pattern Recognition**: Predict object movement
- **Risk vs Reward**: Balance collecting points vs avoiding danger
- **Spatial Awareness**: Navigate in 2D space

## Strategy Tips

- Focus on avoiding asteroids first, collecting second
- Move to planets when safe - they're worth 2.5x stars
- Stay near the center for better reaction time
- Watch the spawn patterns as difficulty increases
