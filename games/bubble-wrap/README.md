# Bubble Wrap Pop

A satisfying bubble wrap popping simulation game for kids!

## Description

Experience the joy of popping bubble wrap without the waste! This game provides a virtual sheet of bubble wrap where kids can tap or click to pop each bubble. It's simple, relaxing, and endlessly satisfying.

## How to Play

1. **Start the Game**: Tap the "Start Popping!" button
2. **Pop the Bubbles**: Tap or click on any unpopped bubble to pop it
3. **Watch Your Progress**: See how many bubbles you've popped and how many remain
4. **Complete the Sheet**: Pop all bubbles to finish
5. **Play Again**: Get a fresh sheet of bubble wrap to pop!

## Features

- **60 Bubbles**: A complete sheet with 10 rows and 6 columns of bubbles
- **Realistic Design**: Bubbles look like real bubble wrap with glossy, 3D appearance
- **Satisfying Animation**: Each pop has a smooth animation effect
- **Score Tracking**: Keep track of popped bubbles and remaining bubbles
- **Mobile-Friendly**: Touch-optimized for tablets and phones
- **Endless Fun**: Generate a new sheet whenever you want

## Controls

- **Touch/Tap**: Pop a bubble (mobile)
- **Click**: Pop a bubble (desktop)

## Game Details

- **Category**: Quick & Simple Game
- **Age Group**: All ages
- **Play Time**: Endless (typically 30-60 seconds per sheet)
- **Educational Value**: Hand-eye coordination, counting, stress relief
