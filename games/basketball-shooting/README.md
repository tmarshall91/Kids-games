# Basketball Shooting 🏀

A fun and engaging basketball shooting game where players aim and shoot hoops to score points!

## How to Play

1. Tap the "Start Game" button to begin
2. Touch and drag down on the basketball to charge your shot
3. The longer you drag, the more power you'll have
4. Release to shoot the ball toward the hoop
5. Score as many baskets as you can in 30 seconds!

## Scoring

- **Regular Basket**: 2 points
- **Swish (Perfect shot)**: 3 points

## Controls

- **Touch/Mouse**: Drag down on the basketball to aim and power up your shot
- **Release**: Let go to shoot the ball

## Features

- Touch-friendly drag-and-shoot mechanics
- Visual power meter to gauge shot strength
- Physics-based ball movement with gravity
- Score tracking with visual feedback
- 30-second timed gameplay
- Responsive design for mobile devices

## Technical Details

- **Technologies**: HTML5, CSS3, Vanilla JavaScript
- **Browser Requirements**: Modern browsers with ES6 support
- **Mobile**: Fully optimized for touch devices
- **Performance**: 60fps smooth animations

## Game Mechanics

The game features realistic physics including:
- Gravity affecting ball trajectory
- Variable shot power based on drag distance
- Collision detection for basket scoring
- Swish detection for perfect shots
