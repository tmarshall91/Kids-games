# Beginning Sounds

A fun and educational game for young children to learn letter sounds by matching pictures with their beginning letters!

## Description

Beginning Sounds helps kids develop phonemic awareness by identifying the first letter of common words. Players see a picture (like an apple) and must tap the correct beginning letter (A) from a multiple-choice selection. Perfect for preschool and early elementary students learning the alphabet and letter sounds.

## How to Play

1. **Start**: Tap the "Start Game" button to begin
2. **Look**: A picture will appear with the word below it
3. **Listen**: Think about the beginning sound of the word
4. **Choose**: Tap the letter that makes that beginning sound
5. **Learn**: See if you're correct with instant feedback
6. **Continue**: Tap "Next" to move to the next round
7. **Complete**: Play all 10 rounds and see your score!

## Game Features

- **26 Items**: One for each letter of the alphabet (A-Z)

## Educational Benefits

## Game Mechanics

### Difficulty
- **Beginner-Friendly**: Only 4 choices per question
- **Fair Selection**: Wrong answers are random letters
- **No Time Pressure**: Players can take their time
- **Encouraging**: Positive feedback regardless of answer

### Scoring
- 1 point per correct answer
- Maximum score: 10 out of 10
- Performance messages based on score percentage:
  - 100%: "Perfect score! You're a letter sound champion!"
  - 80-99%: "Excellent work! You know your letter sounds!"
  - 60-79%: "Good job! Keep practicing!"
  - 40-59%: "Nice effort! You're learning!"
  - 0-39%: "Keep trying! Practice makes perfect!"

### Round System
- 10 total rounds per game
- Random item selection (no repeats until all items used)
- Shows current round (e.g., "Round: 3/10")
- Automatic progression to game over after round 10
