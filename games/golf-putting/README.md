# Golf Putting ⛳

A fun and engaging golf putting game where players sink putts across 9 challenging holes!

## How to Play

1. Tap the "Start Game" button to begin
2. Touch and drag **down** on the golf ball to set your putting power
3. The longer you drag, the more power you'll have
4. Release to putt the ball toward the hole
5. Try to complete all 9 holes with the lowest score!

## Scoring

Golf uses traditional scoring where **lower is better**:

- **Hole in One**: Sink it in one putt! ⛳
- **Eagle**: 2 strokes under par 🦅
- **Birdie**: 1 stroke under par 🐦
- **Par**: Match the target strokes ✓
- **Bogey**: 1 stroke over par +1
- **Double Bogey+**: 2+ strokes over par

Your total score is calculated as strokes relative to par:
- Negative score = Under par (Good!)
- Zero = Even par
- Positive score = Over par
