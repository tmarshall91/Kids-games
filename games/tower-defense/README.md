# 🏰 Tower Defense Kids

A simplified tower defense game perfect for kids! Place towers to defend your castle from waves of cute monsters.

## How to Play

1. **Tap Start** to begin the game
2. **Select a tower** from the bottom menu (Basic 🗼, Fast ⚡, or Strong 💪)
3. **Tap anywhere** on the green area (not on the brown path) to place your tower
4. **Click Next Wave** to start each wave of monsters
5. **Defend your castle** - don't let monsters reach the end!

## Controls

- **Tap/Click**: Place towers on empty spaces
- **Select Tower Type**: Tap the tower buttons at the bottom

## Tower Types

- **🗼 Basic Tower** (50 coins): Balanced damage and fire rate
- **⚡ Fast Tower** (75 coins): Shoots quickly with lower damage
- **💪 Strong Tower** (100 coins): High damage but slower fire rate

## Game Objective

- Stop monsters from reaching your castle
- Earn coins by defeating monsters
- Survive as many waves as possible
- Don't lose all your lives!

## Features

- Three different tower types with unique abilities
- Multiple monster types with different speeds and health
- Progressive difficulty - waves get harder
- Colorful, kid-friendly graphics
- Touch-optimized for mobile devices
- Visual range indicators for towers

## Tips

- Place towers where they can cover more of the path
- Mix different tower types for best results
- Save coins for stronger towers in later waves
- Focus fire on the path curves where monsters slow down
