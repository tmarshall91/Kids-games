# Wildlife Safari Photo Game 📸

An exciting safari adventure where kids tap to photograph wild animals before they disappear!

## How to Play

1. Tap **Start Safari** to begin your adventure
2. Animals will appear randomly on the screen
3. Quickly tap each animal to photograph it
4. Animals disappear after a few seconds if not photographed
5. Photograph as many animals as you can in 30 seconds!

## Features

- **10 different safari animals**: Lion, Elephant, Giraffe, Zebra, Rhino, Leopard, Kangaroo, Gorilla, Crocodile, Parrot
- **30-second timed challenge** for exciting gameplay
- **Beautiful safari scenery** with trees and grass
- **Photo counter** tracking your captures
- **Collection display** showing all animals you photographed
- Smooth animations and visual feedback
- Mobile-optimized touch controls

## Game Mechanics

- Animals spawn randomly across the safari scene
- Each animal stays visible for 2.5 seconds
- Tap quickly to capture the photo
- Timer counts down from 30 seconds
- Track unique animals you've photographed
