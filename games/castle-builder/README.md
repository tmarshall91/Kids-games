# 🏰 Castle Builder

Collect resources and construct your own magnificent medieval castle piece by piece!

## How to Play

1. **Collect Resources**: Tap falling resources to collect them
2. **Gather Materials**: Collect stone 🪨, wood 🪵, and gold 🪙
3. **Build Parts**: Use resources to construct castle components
4. **Complete Castle**: Build all 5 parts to finish your castle
5. **Victory**: Celebrate your completed fortress!

## Resources

- **🪨 Stone** - Common resource for walls and towers (40% spawn rate)
- **🪵 Wood** - Used for towers and gates (40% spawn rate)
- **🪙 Gold** - Rare resource for the flag (20% spawn rate)

## Castle Parts to Build

### Left Tower (🗼)
- **Cost**: 10 Stone + 5 Wood
- **Purpose**: Defensive tower on the left side

### Wall (🧱)
- **Cost**: 15 Stone
- **Purpose**: Main protective wall

### Right Tower (🗼)
- **Cost**: 10 Stone + 5 Wood
- **Purpose**: Defensive tower on the right side

### Gate (🚪)
- **Cost**: 10 Wood
- **Purpose**: Entrance to the castle

### Flag (🚩)
- **Cost**: 5 Gold
- **Purpose**: Victory banner at the top

## Total Resources Needed

To complete the entire castle, you need:
- **Stone**: 35 total (2 towers + wall)
- **Wood**: 20 total (2 towers + gate)
- **Gold**: 5 total (flag)

## Features

- **Resource Collection**: Tap falling items to collect
- **Strategic Building**: Choose which parts to build first
- **Visual Progress**: Watch your castle grow with each part
- **Clear Requirements**: Each button shows required resources
- **Disabled Buttons**: Can't build if you lack resources
- **Victory Screen**: Celebration when castle is complete

## Game Mechanics

- Resources fall every 1.5 seconds
- Fall duration: 4 seconds to collect
- Resources spawn randomly across the field
- Build buttons show resource costs
- Buttons are disabled when you can't afford them
- Already-built parts are marked in green

## Strategy Tips

- Focus on collecting stone early - you need the most
- Don't miss gold coins - they're rare and needed for the flag
- Build strategically based on available resources
- Complete cheaper parts first to see progress
- Watch for falling resources at all times

## Resource Spawn Rates

- Stone: 40% chance
- Wood: 40% chance
- Gold: 20% chance
