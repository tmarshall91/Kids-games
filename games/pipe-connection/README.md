# Pipe Connection

A puzzle game where you rotate pipes to create a continuous path from the water source to the drain!

## How to Play

1. **Goal**: Connect the water source (💧) to the drain (🎯)
2. **Rotate Pipes**: Tap/click on any pipe to rotate it 90 degrees
3. **Check Flow**: When you think you've made a connection, tap "Check Flow"
4. **Complete**: Successfully connect the pipes to advance to the next level

## Game Rules

- The water source is at the top (marked with 💧)
- The drain is at the bottom (marked with 🎯)
- Pipes must be connected end-to-end to create a path
- Each tap rotates a pipe by 90 degrees
- Try to complete each level with fewer moves!

## Features

- Progressive difficulty with 5 levels
- Grid sizes increase from 5x5 to 7x7
- Visual feedback when pipes are connected
- Move counter to track your efficiency
- Touch-optimized for mobile devices
- Responsive design for all screen sizes

## Pipe Types

- **Straight Pipes**: Connect two opposite sides
- **Corner Pipes**: Connect two adjacent sides
- **Source**: Starting point for the water
- **Drain**: Target destination

## Controls

**Mobile & Desktop:**
- Tap/click any pipe to rotate it clockwise
- Tap "Check Flow" to test your connection
- Tap "Restart" to reset the current level
- Tap "Next Level" after completing a level

## Strategy Tips

- Start from the source and work toward the drain
- Look for the natural flow path before rotating
- Corner pieces are key to changing direction
- Don't rush - fewer moves is better!
- Use "Check Flow" to test your progress
