# Sticker Scene Creator 🎪

Build creative scenes by dragging and dropping fun stickers!

## How to Play

1. **Choose Stickers**: Tap any sticker from the panel
2. **Place Stickers**: They appear in random positions
3. **Move Stickers**: Drag them anywhere on the scene
4. **Remove Stickers**: Double-tap/double-click to remove
5. **Build Scenes**: Create parks, cities, or anything you imagine!

## Features

- **15 Stickers**: Animals, nature, and objects
- **Drag & Drop**: Move stickers anywhere
- **Sky & Grass Background**: Perfect scene backdrop
- **Free Placement**: Unlimited stickers
- **Easy Removal**: Double-tap to delete stickers

## Sticker Categories

- **Animals**: Dog, cat, lion, elephant, butterfly
- **Nature**: Tree, flower, sun, star, rainbow
- **Objects**: House, car, ball, balloon, gift

## Technical Details

- DOM-based sticker system
- Touch and mouse drag support
- Boundary detection
- Z-index management for overlapping
