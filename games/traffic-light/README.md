# Traffic Light Game 🚦

A fun reaction time game for kids! Test how fast you can respond when the traffic light turns green.

## 🎮 How to Play

1. **Tap "Start Game"** to begin
2. Watch the traffic light carefully:
   - 🟡 **YELLOW** = Get Ready!
   - 🔴 **RED** = Don't Tap! (Wait for green)
   - 🟢 **GREEN** = TAP AS FAST AS YOU CAN!
3. The game has **10 rounds**
4. Try to get the fastest reaction time and highest score!

## 🎯 Objective

- Tap the traffic light as quickly as possible when it turns GREEN
- Avoid tapping when it's RED (you'll lose points!)
- The faster you react, the more points you earn

## 📊 Scoring System

- **Green Light Tap**: 10-100 points (faster reactions = more points)
  - Under 200ms = ~80-100 points
  - 200-500ms = ~50-80 points
  - Over 500ms = ~10-50 points
- **Red Light Tap**: -100 points (penalty)
- **Too Slow (over 5 seconds)**: -50 points
- **Best Reaction Time**: Tracked and displayed!

## 🎨 Game Mechanics

Each round follows this sequence:
1. Yellow light appears (1 second warning)
2. Red light appears (random 1.5-3.5 seconds)
3. Green light appears (TAP NOW!)
4. Your reaction time is measured
5. Points are awarded based on speed
6. Move to next round

## 🏆 Tips for High Scores

- Focus on the traffic light
- Stay relaxed but ready
- Don't anticipate - wait for the green!
- Quick taps when green = more points
- Avoid tapping on red to prevent penalties
