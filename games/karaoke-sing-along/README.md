# Karaoke Sing-Along 🎤

Sing along with the lyrics! Follow the words as they highlight and have fun singing your favorite songs.

## How to Play

1. **Choose a song** from the list
2. Tap **Start Singing** to begin
3. **Follow the highlighted lyrics**
4. **Sing along** as words light up!
5. Have fun and practice your singing!

## Controls

- **Tap/Click**: Navigate songs
- **Play/Pause**: Control playback

## Features

- **Multiple Songs**: Selection of kid-friendly songs
- **Highlighted Lyrics**: Words light up in rhythm
- **Easy to Follow**: Large, clear text
- **Practice Mode**: Repeat favorite parts
- **Tempo Display**: See the rhythm
