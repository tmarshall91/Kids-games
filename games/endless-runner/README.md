# Endless Runner

An auto-scrolling endless runner game where you jump and duck to avoid obstacles!

## How to Play

1. Tap "Start Running" to begin
2. Tap the **top half** of the screen to **jump** over ground obstacles
3. Tap the **bottom half** of the screen to **duck** under flying obstacles
4. The game speed increases as you progress
5. Avoid all obstacles to survive as long as possible
6. Try to beat your high score!

## Features

- Auto-scrolling gameplay
- Two types of obstacles: ground and air
- Simple tap controls for jump and duck
- Progressive difficulty (speed increases over time)
- Score tracking with high score persistence
- Smooth animations
- Responsive design for mobile devices

## Technical Details

- **Technologies**: HTML5 Canvas, CSS3, Vanilla JavaScript
- **Browser Requirements**: Modern mobile browsers (iOS Safari 12+, Chrome 80+)
- **Controls**: Touch/Click (top half = jump, bottom half = duck)
- **Storage**: Uses localStorage for high score persistence
