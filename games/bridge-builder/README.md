# Bridge Builder

A physics-based construction game where you build bridges to help a car cross safely from one cliff to another!

## How to Play

1. **Build**: Draw beams and cables by clicking/tapping between anchor points
2. **Budget**: Stay within your budget - beams cost $10, cables cost $5
3. **Test**: Click "Test Bridge" to see if your bridge can hold the car
4. **Win**: Successfully get the car across to complete the level

## Game Rules

- Click/tap and drag between anchor points to create structures
- **Beams** ($10): Strong horizontal supports that the car drives on
- **Cables** ($5): Lighter support structures for reinforcement
- The car must reach the other side without the bridge collapsing
- Each level has a limited budget
- Structures will show stress indicators when tested

## Features

- 5 progressively challenging levels
- Increasing gap distances and budgets
- Simple physics simulation with gravity and stress
- Visual feedback for structural integrity
- Touch and mouse support
- Budget management gameplay

## Building Tips

- Create a solid foundation with beams for the car to drive on
- Use cables to provide support at lower cost
- Triangular structures are typically stronger
- Don't forget to connect to both cliffs!
- Test your design early to see what works

## Controls

**Building:**
- Click/tap and drag between anchor points to create structures
- Select "Beam" or "Cable" tool before drawing
- Click "Clear" to remove all structures and start over

**Testing:**
- Click "Test Bridge" to start the simulation
- Watch as the car drives across your bridge
- If successful, proceed to the next level
- If failed, click "Try Again" to rebuild

## Strategy

- **Strong Base**: Build horizontal beams for the car path
- **Support Structure**: Add diagonal cables for stability
- **Budget Wisely**: Balance cost and strength
- **Test Early**: Don't spend your whole budget before testing
- **Learn from Failure**: Each collapse teaches you something!

## Physics

- **Gravity**: The car falls if not supported
- **Stress**: Structures accumulate stress from the car's weight
- **Failure**: Structures break if stress exceeds their limit
- **Success**: Car reaches the right cliff safely
