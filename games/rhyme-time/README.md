# Rhyme Time

A fun and educational game for kids to learn and practice identifying rhyming words! Players are presented with a target word and must choose which word from multiple options rhymes with it.

## How to Play

1. **Start the Game**: Tap the "Start Game" button to begin
2. **Read the Target Word**: Look at the word displayed at the top (e.g., "CAT")
3. **Find the Rhyme**: Choose the word that rhymes with the target word from the 4 options below
4. **Get Feedback**: The game will show you if you're correct and highlight the right answer
5. **Continue Playing**: Tap "Next Word" to move to the next round
6. **Complete 10 Rounds**: Play through all 10 rounds to see your final score and star rating!

## Features

- **12 Rhyming Word Sets**: Includes common rhyming pairs like CAT/HAT, DOG/FROG, BEE/TREE, and more
- **Multiple Choice Format**: 4 options per round with only one correct rhyming word
- **Visual Feedback**:
  - Correct answers show in green with a pulse animation
  - Wrong answers shake and show in red
  - The correct answer is always highlighted
- **Score Tracking**: Keep track of correct answers out of 10 rounds
- **Star Rating System**:
  - ⭐⭐⭐ (3 stars): 90% or higher
  - ⭐⭐ (2 stars): 70-89%
  - ⭐ (1 star): 50-69%
  - ✨ Keep practicing: Below 50%
- **Responsive Design**: Works perfectly on phones, tablets, and desktops
- **Touch-Friendly**: Large buttons (60px minimum) optimized for small fingers
- **Colorful Interface**: Engaging purple gradient theme that kids love
