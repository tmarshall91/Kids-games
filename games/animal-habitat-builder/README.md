# Animal Habitat Builder 🏡🌳

Create beautiful and perfect habitats for different animals with trees, water, food, and structures!

## How to Play

1. **Choose an animal** from the animal palette at the bottom
2. **Select elements** from the items palette (trees, water, food, etc.)
3. **Tap on the canvas** to place items where you want them
4. **Build the perfect home** for your animal
5. **Save your habitat** when you're done!

## Features

- **6 animals** to build for: Lion, Elephant, Bear, Fox, Panda, Giraffe
- **30+ habitat elements**:
  - Trees and plants (🌳🌲🌴🌵🌿🍃🌺🌸🌻)
  - Water features (💧🌊💦)
  - Structures (🏠🏡⛺🏔️🪨)
  - Food (🍎🍌🥕🥜🍯🐟)
- **Free placement** - tap anywhere to place items
- **Long-press to remove** items (mobile)
- **Clear all** button to start over
- **Save your creation** and view a preview
- Unlimited creativity!

## Controls

**Placing Items:**
- Select an item from the palette
- Tap on the canvas where you want it
- Repeat to add more items

**Removing Items:**
- Mobile: Long-press on an item (0.5 seconds)
- Desktop: Shift+Click or Ctrl+Click on an item

**Other:**
- Clear All: Remove everything and start fresh
- Save Habitat: Capture and view your creation

## Creative Tips

- Layer items for depth
- Create themed habitats (forest, desert, arctic)
- Add food sources for your animals
- Include water features
- Build shelters and caves
- Make it colorful with flowers
