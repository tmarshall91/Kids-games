# Sight Word Practice

A fast-paced educational game designed to help children practice quick recognition of common sight words using the Dolch word list. Perfect for building reading fluency and automaticity in word recognition.

## Game Overview

Sight Word Practice presents children with high-frequency words and challenges them to quickly identify each word from multiple-choice options. The game rewards both accuracy and speed, encouraging rapid word recognition skills essential for reading fluency.

## Educational Benefits

- **Word Recognition**: Develops automatic recognition of high-frequency sight words
- **Reading Fluency**: Builds speed and confidence in reading common words
- **Visual Processing**: Improves quick visual identification of word patterns
- **Memory Reinforcement**: Strengthens word memory through repeated exposure
- **Reading Confidence**: Builds confidence with words that appear frequently in texts

## Game Features

### Core Mechanics
- **20 Rapid Rounds**: Fast-paced gameplay with 20 word recognition challenges
- **Multiple Choice**: 4 answer options for each word displayed
- **Dolch Sight Words**: Uses the proven Dolch word list (Pre-Primer through First Grade)
- **Speed Bonuses**: Extra points for answering within 3 seconds
- **Visual Feedback**: Immediate feedback showing correct answers

### Scoring System
- **10 points** for each correct answer
- **5 bonus points** for fast responses (under 3 seconds)
- Maximum possible score: **300 points** (20 rounds × 15 points each)

### Progress Tracking
- **Score Counter**: Real-time score updates
- **Round Counter**: Shows current round out of 20
- **Timer**: Displays total elapsed time
- **Progress Bar**: Visual indicator of game completion
- **Accuracy Percentage**: Final accuracy displayed at game end

## How to Play

1. **Start Game**: Tap the "Start Game" button to begin
2. **Read the Word**: A sight word appears in large, clear text
3. **Choose Answer**: Select the correct word from 4 options
4. **Get Feedback**: Instant feedback shows if you're correct
5. **Continue Playing**: Complete all 20 rounds
6. **View Results**: See your final score, accuracy, and time

## Word List

The game uses the Dolch Sight Word List, covering:
- **Pre-Primer**: 40 basic words (the, and, is, it, in, etc.)
- **Primer**: 52 common words (all, am, are, at, be, etc.)
- **First Grade**: 41 frequently used words (after, again, an, any, etc.)

Total: **133 sight words** that make up 50-75% of all written English.

## Tips for Parents and Teachers

### Best Practices
1. **Regular Practice**: Short, daily sessions (5-10 minutes) are most effective
2. **Encourage Speed**: Remind children that fast recognition is the goal
3. **Celebrate Progress**: Focus on improvement rather than perfection
4. **Mix with Reading**: Use game as supplement to regular reading practice
5. **Track Growth**: Note accuracy improvements over multiple sessions

### Difficulty Levels
The game automatically mixes words from different levels:
- **Beginners**: Focus on getting words right (accuracy over speed)
- **Intermediate**: Aim for 80%+ accuracy with improving speed
- **Advanced**: Try for perfect score with speed bonuses

### Learning Progression
- **Week 1-2**: Focus on accuracy, build familiarity with words
- **Week 3-4**: Start working on speed, aim for bonuses
- **Ongoing**: Maintain skills, track consistent accuracy

## Game Statistics

Players can track their performance through:
- **Final Score**: Total points earned (out of 300)
- **Accuracy**: Percentage of words correctly identified
- **Total Time**: How long it took to complete all 20 rounds
- **Performance Message**: Encouraging feedback based on results

### Performance Ratings
- **100% accuracy**: "Perfect! You're a sight word master!"
- **80-99% accuracy**: "Excellent work! Keep practicing!"
- **60-79% accuracy**: "Good job! You're improving!"
- **Below 60%**: "Nice try! Practice makes perfect!"
