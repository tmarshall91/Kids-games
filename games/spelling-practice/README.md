# Spelling Practice

Look at the emoji picture and spell the word! Practice spelling with visual cues.

## How to Play

1. Tap "Start Game" to begin
2. Look at the emoji picture
3. Tap letters on the keyboard to spell the word
4. Tap "Check Answer" to see if you're correct
5. Continue through 10 rounds
6. Try to spell as many words correctly as possible!

## Features

- Emoji-based visual cues
- Touch-optimized keyboard
- 10 rounds per game
- Immediate feedback on answers
- Score tracking
- Clear button to fix mistakes
- Responsive design

## Technical Details

- Pure HTML5, CSS3, and Vanilla JavaScript
- Mobile-first design
- No external images needed (uses emojis)
- Touch and mouse support
- Works offline

## Words

Includes common animals: cat, dog, fish, bear, lion, elephant, giraffe, monkey, zebra, penguin
