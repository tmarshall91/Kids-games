# Whack-a-Mole

A classic arcade-style game where players tap moles before they disappear back into their holes. Test your reflexes and aim for a high score!

## How to Play

1. **Tap "Start Game"** to begin
2. **Tap the moles** (🦦) as they pop up from the holes
3. **Score points** - Each mole you hit is worth 10 points
4. **Race against time** - You have 30 seconds to score as many points as possible
5. **Try to beat your high score!**

### Controls

- **Touch/Tap**: Hit the moles on mobile devices
- **Click**: Hit the moles on desktop

### Scoring

- Each mole hit: **10 points**
- Score rankings:
  - 300+ points: Mole Master! 🏆
  - 200-299 points: Awesome reflexes! ⭐
  - 100-199 points: Well done! 👍
  - 50-99 points: Good effort! 👏
  - 0-49 points: Keep practicing! 💪

## Features

- **Mobile-first design** - Optimized for touch screens
- **Responsive layout** - Works on all device sizes
- **Smooth animations** - Moles pop up and down smoothly
- **Score tracking** - See your score in real-time
- **Timer countdown** - 30-second game sessions
- **Multiple active moles** - Up to 2 moles can be active at once
- **Progressive difficulty** - Moles appear at random intervals
- **Visual feedback** - Score popups and animations when you hit moles
- **Game over screen** - Shows final score and personalized message
