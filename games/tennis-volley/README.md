# Tennis Volley 🎾

A fun timing-based tennis game where you tap to hit the ball back and forth! Perfect your timing to keep the rally going as long as possible.

## 🎮 How to Play

1. **Start the Game**: Tap the "Start Game" button to begin
2. **Watch the Ball**: The ball will bounce toward you from the opponent's side
3. **Time Your Hit**: Tap when the ball enters your zone (bottom half of the court)
4. **Keep the Rally Going**: The longer you can keep hitting the ball, the higher your rally score!
5. **Perfect Your Timing**:
   - **Perfect Hit** 🌟: Tap at just the right moment for the best timing
   - **Good Hit** ✓: Decent timing keeps the rally going
   - **Miss** ✗: Too early, too late, or missed completely ends the game

## ✨ Game Features

### Visual Elements
- 🎾 **Tennis Ball**: Bounces back and forth across the court
- 🏸 **Rackets**: You and your opponent ready to hit
- 🌟 **Tennis Court**: Green court with realistic net
- 📊 **Timing Indicator**: Shows when you should tap for perfect timing
- 💬 **Feedback Messages**: Instant feedback on your timing quality

### Gameplay Mechanics
- **Rally Counter**: Tracks your current rally streak
- **Best Rally**: Saves your longest rally (stored locally)
- **Progressive Difficulty**: Ball speed increases with each successful hit
- **Timing System**: Three levels of timing (Perfect, Good, Miss)
- **Visual Feedback**: Animations and messages for each hit
- **Auto-Opponent**: Opponent automatically returns the ball

### Scoring
- Each successful hit increases your rally count by 1
- The game tracks your best rally across sessions
- Try to beat your personal best!

## 🎯 Tips for Success

1. **Watch the Timing Indicator**: The green zone shows the perfect timing window
2. **Don't Tap Too Early**: Wait for the ball to get close to your side
3. **Stay Focused**: The ball gets faster as you progress
4. **Practice Makes Perfect**: Keep playing to improve your timing
5. **Aim for Perfect Hits**: Better timing helps you build longer rallies

## 🐛 Troubleshooting

**Ball not moving?**
- Make sure you've pressed "Start Game"
- Try refreshing the page

**Can't tap the ball?**
- Wait for the ball to reach your zone (bottom half)
- Watch for the timing indicator
- Make sure the game has started

**Best rally not saving?**
- Check if your browser allows localStorage
- Try clearing browser cache and replaying
