# Rhythm Tapping 🎵

Tap to the rhythm! Follow the beat and tap in perfect timing to score points and build streaks.

## How to Play

1. Tap **Start Tapping** to begin
2. Watch the **pulsing circles** - they show the rhythm
3. **Tap the center** when the beat hits (circles are smallest)
4. Build your **streak** by tapping accurately
5. Three misses and you're out!

## Controls

- **Tap/Click**: Tap the center circle in rhythm

## Objective

- Tap in time with the beat
- **Perfect timing**: Tap within 200ms of the beat (20+ points)
- **Good timing**: Tap within 400ms of the beat (10+ points)
- **Off beat**: Miss the timing (streak resets, counts as miss)
- Build streaks for bonus points!

## Features

- **Visual Beat Indicator**: Pulsing rings show the rhythm
- **Streak System**: Chain perfect taps for bonus points
- **Timing Feedback**: See how well you tapped
- **Audio Cues**: Hear the beat and your performance
- **Progressive Challenge**: Build your rhythm skills

## Scoring

- **Perfect Tap**: 20 points + (streak × 2) bonus
- **Good Tap**: 10 points + streak bonus
- **Miss**: 0 points, streak resets

Example:
- 1st Perfect: 20 points
- 2nd Perfect: 22 points (20 + 1×2)
- 3rd Perfect: 24 points (20 + 2×2)
- 4th Perfect: 26 points (20 + 3×2)

## Game Over

- You get **3 misses** before game over
- Try to build the longest streak possible!

## Tips

- **Watch AND listen** - use both visual and audio cues
- **Feel the rhythm** - let the beat guide you
- **Don't rush** - timing is more important than speed
- **Stay relaxed** - tension affects timing
- **Practice** - your rhythm will improve!

## Strategy

- Count along with the beat (1-2-1-2)
- Tap slightly before you think the beat hits
- Use the visual rings as guides
- Close your eyes once you feel the rhythm
- Build confidence with perfect taps
