# Rhythm Tapping

Test your rhythm and timing by tapping along with the beat! Keep in sync to build streaks and achieve high accuracy.

## How to Play

1. Tap "Start Tapping" to begin
2. Watch and listen to the metronome beat (top circle pulses)
3. Tap the large green circle in rhythm with each beat
4. Try to tap right when you hear/see the beat
5. Build streaks by staying on rhythm
6. Game lasts 45 seconds

## Scoring System

**Timing Windows:**
- **Perfect**: Tap within 100ms of the beat (best timing)
- **Good**: Tap within 200ms of the beat
- **Miss**: Tap outside the timing window or miss a beat

**Accuracy Calculation:**
- Accuracy % = (Perfect + Good taps) / Total taps × 100

**Streak System:**
- Each Perfect or Good tap increases your streak
- Missing resets your streak to 0
- Try to build the longest streak possible!

## Game Features

- **Visual Metronome**: Pulsing circle shows the beat
- **Audio Feedback**: Hear each beat clearly
- **Real-time Feedback**: See "Perfect!", "Good!", or "Miss!" for each tap
- **Accuracy Tracking**: Live accuracy percentage
- **Streak Counter**: Current streak and best streak
- **45-Second Challenge**: Quick gameplay sessions

- **Tempo**: 120 BPM (beats per minute)

## Learning Benefits

- **Rhythm Development**: Develop internal sense of rhythm and timing
- **Musical Timing**: Learn to stay on beat
- **Concentration**: Maintain focus through the entire challenge
- **Coordination**: Sync visual and audio cues with physical tapping
- **Pattern Recognition**: Anticipate the regular beat pattern

## Tips for High Accuracy

- Listen to the audio beat rather than just watching
- Try to feel the rhythm internally
- Don't tap too quickly - wait for the beat
- Relax and don't overthink it
- The perfect timing is right when the visual pulse happens
- Practice makes perfect!

## Difficulty

The game maintains a steady 120 BPM tempo throughout, but the challenge comes from:
- Maintaining accuracy for 45 seconds
- Building and keeping long streaks
- Achieving high accuracy percentage
