# Sound Matching

A musical memory game where you match pairs of sounds! Tap cards to reveal sounds and find matching pairs.

## How to Play

1. Tap "Start Game" to begin
2. Tap any card to hear its sound
3. Tap another card to hear its sound
4. If the sounds match, the cards stay revealed
5. If they don't match, cards flip back
6. Match all 8 pairs to win!

## Game Features

- **8 Musical Notes**: Do, Re, Mi, Fa, Sol, La, Ti, Do (octave)
- **Memory Challenge**: Remember which sounds are where
- **Visual Icons**: Each sound has a unique musical icon
- **Try Counter**: Track how many attempts you make
- **Win Condition**: Match all pairs to complete the game

## The Sounds

Each card plays a specific musical note:
- 🎵 Do (C4) - 261.63 Hz
- 🎶 Re (D4) - 293.66 Hz
- 🎼 Mi (E4) - 329.63 Hz
- 🎹 Fa (F4) - 349.23 Hz
- 🎸 Sol (G4) - 392.00 Hz
- 🎺 La (A4) - 440.00 Hz
- 🎻 Ti (B4) - 493.88 Hz
- 🥁 Do (C5) - 523.25 Hz

## Game Mechanics

- 16 cards total (8 pairs)
- Cards are shuffled randomly each game
- Can only flip 2 cards at a time
- Matched pairs stay revealed with green highlight
- Wrong matches shake and flip back
- Count your tries to beat your best score

## Learning Benefits

- **Memory Development**: Strengthen short-term memory
- **Musical Ear Training**: Learn to distinguish different musical notes
- **Concentration**: Improve focus and attention
- **Pattern Recognition**: Remember locations and sounds
- **Auditory Processing**: Develop ability to identify and match sounds

## Strategy Tips

- Try to remember card positions
- Focus on the sound, not just the icon
- Work systematically (don't randomly tap)
- Keep track of unmatched cards
- Practice makes perfect - replay to improve!

## Scoring

- Lower try count is better
- Perfect game: 8 tries (match each pair on first attempt)
- Challenge yourself to improve your try count

## Difficulty

The game is straightforward but challenging because:
- You must remember both sound and position
- Similar notes can be confusing
- 16 cards provide good challenge without being overwhelming
