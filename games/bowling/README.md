# 🎳 Bowling

A fun and interactive bowling game for kids! Roll the ball, knock down pins, and score strikes and spares across 10 frames of bowling action.

## 🎮 How to Play

1. **Aim Your Shot**: Touch and drag the bowling ball left or right to aim
2. **Roll the Ball**: Swipe up to release and roll the ball down the lane
3. **Knock Down Pins**: Try to knock down all 10 pins arranged in a triangle
4. **Score Points**: Get strikes (all pins in one ball) or spares (all pins in two balls)
5. **Complete 10 Frames**: Play through all 10 frames to finish the game

## 🎯 Game Features

### Bowling Mechanics
- **Touch Controls**: Swipe left/right to aim, swipe up to roll
- **Visual Aiming Guide**: Yellow line shows where your ball will go
- **Realistic Pin Physics**: Pins react to ball collisions and chain reactions
- **Pin Arrangement**: Standard 10-pin triangular formation

### Scoring System
- **Regular Scoring**: Each pin knocked down = points
- **Strike**: Knock down all 10 pins with the first ball = bonus points! 🎳
- **Spare**: Knock down all 10 pins with two balls = bonus points! 🎯
- **10 Frames**: Standard bowling game with 10 frames
- **Score Tracking**: Running total score with frame-by-frame breakdown

### Visual Feedback
- **Strike/Spare Notifications**: Big popups when you score special shots
- **Pin Animations**: Pins fall when knocked down
- **Rolling Ball Animation**: Ball spins as it rolls down the lane
- **Score Breakdown**: See all your frames and results at game end

## 🏆 Scoring Explained

### Basic Scoring
- Each pin knocked down counts as 1 point
- You get up to 2 balls per frame (except strikes)

### Strike (X)
- Knock down all 10 pins with your first ball
- Score: 10 + your next 2 rolls
- Big bonus points!

### Spare (/)
- Knock down all 10 pins using both balls
- Score: 10 + your next 1 roll
- Good bonus points!

### Regular Frame
- Total pins knocked with both balls
- No bonus

### Example Scores
- **Perfect Game**: 200+ points (all strikes)
- **Great Game**: 150+ points
- **Good Game**: 100+ points
- **Keep Practicing**: Under 100 points

## 🎯 Tips for High Scores

1. **Aim for the Center**: The middle pin area gives best strike chances
2. **Swipe Smoothly**: A steady upward swipe gives better control
3. **Watch the Guide**: Use the yellow aiming line to plan your shot
4. **Go for Strikes**: Strikes give the biggest score bonuses
5. **Complete Spares**: If you miss the strike, try for the spare!
