# 🌱 Garden Growing

A fun and educational gardening game where kids can plant seeds, watch them grow, and harvest crops!

## How to Play

1. **Select a Seed**: Choose from tomatoes 🍅, carrots 🥕, corn 🌽, or flowers 🌻
2. **Plant**: Tap any empty plot to plant your selected seed
3. **Watch**: Plants grow through 3 stages: seedling 🌱 → growing 🌿 → ready to harvest
4. **Harvest**: Tap fully grown plants to harvest them and earn points
5. **Score**: Different plants give different points:
   - Tomatoes: 10 points
   - Carrots: 15 points
   - Corn: 20 points
   - Flowers: 25 points

## Features

- **3x3 Garden Grid**: 9 plots to manage your garden
- **4 Plant Types**: Each with unique point values
- **Growth Animation**: Watch your plants grow in real-time
- **Score Tracking**: Track points and total crops harvested
- **Timed Gameplay**: 60 seconds of gardening fun
- **Touch Optimized**: Perfect for mobile devices

## Game Mechanics

- Plants take 3 seconds to fully grow
- Each plant goes through 3 growth stages
- Harvest timing is strategic - balance planting new crops vs. harvesting ready ones
- Higher point crops (flowers) are more valuable but require the same time to grow
