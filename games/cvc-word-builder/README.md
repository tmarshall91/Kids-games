# CVC Word Builder

A fun and educational phonics game that helps early readers learn CVC (Consonant-Vowel-Consonant) word patterns.

## Game Description

CVC Word Builder is an interactive game designed for children learning to read. Players see a picture clue and must build a simple three-letter word by selecting the correct consonant, vowel, and consonant in sequence. This game reinforces fundamental phonics skills and helps children understand basic word construction.

## How to Play

1. **Start the Game**: Click the "Start Game" button to begin
2. **View the Clue**: Look at the emoji picture clue that represents the CVC word
3. **Build the Word**:
   - First, select the beginning consonant from 5 options
   - Then, select the middle vowel from 5 options
   - Finally, select the ending consonant from 5 options
4. **Check Your Answer**: After selecting all three letters, click "Check Word" to see if you're correct
5. **Continue Playing**: Play through 10 rounds and try to get the highest score!

## Educational Benefits

- **Phonics Skills**: Reinforces understanding of consonant and vowel sounds
- **Word Recognition**: Builds familiarity with common CVC words
- **Pattern Recognition**: Helps children understand the C-V-C word structure
- **Reading Readiness**: Develops essential pre-reading and early reading skills
- **Visual Association**: Connects written words with picture representations

- **20 CVC Words**: Including cat, dog, bat, hat, pig, hen, sun, cup, bug, and more

## Tips for Parents and Teachers

1. **Start Simple**: Let children become familiar with the game mechanics first
2. **Sound It Out**: Encourage children to say each letter sound as they select it
3. **Blend Sounds**: Help children blend the three sounds together (e.g., /k/ - /a/ - /t/ = cat)
4. **Celebrate Progress**: Focus on learning rather than just scoring points
5. **Practice Regularly**: Short, frequent practice sessions work best
6. **Extend Learning**: Use physical objects or drawings to reinforce the words
