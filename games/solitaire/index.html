How to Play

  • 🎯 Move all cards to the foundation piles (top right)
  • 🏠 Foundation piles build up by suit (A→2→3...→K)
  • 📚 Tableau piles build down, alternating colors
  • 🃏 Tap the deck to draw new cards
  • ✨ Tap a card, then tap where to move it
  • 🎉 Win by completing all four foundation piles!
