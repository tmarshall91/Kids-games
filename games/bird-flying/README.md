# Bird Flying Game 🐦

A fun flying adventure where kids help a bird navigate through obstacles by tapping to make it fly!

## How to Play

1. Tap **Start Flying** to begin
2. Tap anywhere on the screen to make the bird flap and fly up
3. The bird will fall due to gravity when you don't tap
4. Navigate through the gaps between obstacles
5. Try to get the highest score possible!

## Features

- **Simple one-tap controls** - Easy for kids to learn
- **Progressive difficulty** - Obstacles keep coming
- **High score tracking** - Beat your personal best
- **Smooth physics** - Realistic bird movement
- **Beautiful sky scenery** with clouds
- Score counter with visual feedback
- Mobile-optimized touch controls

## Game Mechanics

- Tap to make the bird flap upward
- Gravity pulls the bird down
- Navigate through gaps in obstacles
- Each obstacle passed earns 1 point
- Hit an obstacle or ground = game over
- High scores are saved automatically
