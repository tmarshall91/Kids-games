# Soccer Penalty Kicks ⚽

An exciting penalty kick game where players aim their shots to beat the goalkeeper and score goals!

## How to Play

1. Tap the "Start Game" button to begin
2. You'll get 10 penalty kicks
3. Tap one of the six target zones to aim your shot
4. Watch as the ball flies toward the goal
5. Try to beat the goalkeeper and score as many goals as possible!

## Scoring

- You have 10 total shots
- Score as many goals as you can
- The goalkeeper will try to save your shots!

## Controls

- **Touch/Click**: Tap any target zone to aim and shoot

## Target Zones

- **Top Corners**: Harder for keeper to reach
- **Middle Zones**: Moderate difficulty
- **Bottom Corners**: Easier shots but keeper can save

## Features

- 6 different target zones to aim for
- Animated goalkeeper with diving mechanics
- Dynamic shot animations
- Score tracking across 10 shots
- Goalkeeper AI that attempts saves
- Responsive design for mobile devices
