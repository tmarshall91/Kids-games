# Farm Animal Sounds 🐄🐷

Learn what sounds farm animals make through interactive play and quizzes!

## How to Play

### Free Play Mode
1. Tap on any animal card to see and hear its sound
2. Each card shows the animal's emoji, name, and sound
3. Explore all 10 different farm animals
4. Learn at your own pace

### Quiz Mode
1. Switch to **Quiz Mode** using the button
2. You'll see a sound (e.g., "Moo!")
3. Tap the animal that makes that sound
4. Answer 10 questions to complete the quiz
5. See your score and try to get 100%!

## Features

- **10 farm animals**: Cow, Pig, Chicken, Horse, Sheep, Goat, Duck, Dog, Cat, Rooster
- **Two game modes**: Free Play for learning, Quiz for testing
- **Visual feedback** with animations
- **Sound text display** showing what each animal says
- **Quiz scoring** with percentage and feedback
- Educational and fun for kids

## Game Modes

**Free Play:**
- No time limits or pressure
- Tap any animal to see its sound
- Perfect for younger kids learning

**Quiz Mode:**
- 10 questions per quiz
- Multiple choice with 4 options
- Shows correct answer if you're wrong
- Score tracking and percentage
