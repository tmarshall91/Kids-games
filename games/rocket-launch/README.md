# Rocket Launch Sequence 🚀

An exciting space exploration game where kids prepare and launch a rocket to reach the stars!

## How to Play

1. Tap **Start Mission** to begin your space adventure
2. **Fuel the Rocket**: Tap the fuel button repeatedly to fill up the tank to 100%
3. **System Check**: Once fueled, run a system check to ensure everything is ready
4. **Launch**: When all systems are go, tap the LAUNCH button!
5. Watch the countdown and blast off to space!
6. Your rocket will fly as long as it has fuel

## Mission Objectives

Reach different milestones in space:
- **100 km**: Escape the atmosphere ☁️
- **400 km**: Reach orbit around Earth 🌙
- **800 km**: Enter deep space 🌟

## Scoring

- Earn points for every kilometer traveled
- The higher you go, the better your score!
- Try to reach deep space for maximum points

## Features

- Interactive launch sequence with countdown
- Real-time fuel consumption
- Beautiful space visuals with planets
- Multiple achievement levels
- Touch-optimized controls

## Tips

- Make sure to fuel up completely before launching
- Watch your fuel gauge during flight
- Try to beat your previous altitude record!
