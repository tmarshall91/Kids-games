# Beat Making 🎛️

Create your own beats! Use a simple sequencer to make music patterns with different drums and instruments.

## How to Play

1. Tap **Start Creating** to begin
2. **Tap grid squares** to place beats
3. Each **row** is a different instrument
4. **Columns** represent different times in the beat
5. Press **Play** to hear your creation!

## Controls

- **Tap/Click**: Toggle beats on/off in the grid
- **Play**: Listen to your beat
- **Clear**: Start over
- **Speed**: Adjust tempo

## Features

- **4 Instruments**: Kick, Snare, Hi-Hat, Clap
- **16-Step Sequencer**: Create complex patterns
- **Tempo Control**: Adjust speed
- **Loop Playback**: Hear your beat repeat
- **Save & Load**: Keep your creations
