# Beat Making

Create your own music beats and loops using a step sequencer! Perfect for budding music producers and beat makers.

## How to Play

1. Tap cells in the grid to add drum sounds to your beat
2. Each row represents a different drum sound (Kick, Snare, Hi-Hat, Clap)
3. Each column represents a 16th note in time
4. Press "Play" to hear your beat loop
5. Adjust the tempo slider to speed up or slow down
6. Press "Clear" to start over

## Instruments

- **Kick**: Deep bass drum (bottom-heavy sound)
- **Snare**: Sharp snare drum (crisp hit)
- **Hi-Hat**: Cymbal chick sound (high frequency)
- **Clap**: Hand clap sound (percussive accent)

## Features

- **16-Step Sequencer**: Create patterns up to 16 steps
- **4 Drum Sounds**: Kick, snare, hi-hat, and clap
- **Tempo Control**: Adjust speed from 60 to 180 BPM
- **Visual Feedback**: See your beat play in real-time
- **Loop Playback**: Beat repeats automatically
- **Instant Preview**: Hear sounds when you tap cells

## Game Mechanics

- Grid has 4 rows (instruments) × 16 columns (time steps)
- Active cells (green) will play their sound when reached
- Visual playback indicator shows current position
- Beat loops continuously when playing
- Each column is a 16th note subdivision

## Beat Making Tips

**Basic Patterns:**
- Simple: Kick on beats 1 and 3, snare on beats 2 and 4
- Dance: Kick on all 4 beats, hi-hat on every 8th note
- Hip-Hop: Kick-clap-kick-clap with hi-hat fills

**Experiment With:**
- Off-beat patterns
- Syncopated rhythms
- Different hi-hat patterns
- Clap variations

## Common Beat Patterns

**Four-on-the-Floor (Dance):**
- Kick: 1, 5, 9, 13
- Snare: 5, 13
- Hi-Hat: All 16 steps

**Hip-Hop:**
- Kick: 1, 7, 13
- Snare: 5, 13
- Hi-Hat: 3, 7, 11, 15
