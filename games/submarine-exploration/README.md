# Submarine Exploration 🔱

Dive deep into the ocean and discover amazing sea creatures in this underwater adventure!

## How to Play

1. Tap **Start Exploration** to begin your dive
2. Use the arrow buttons (or keyboard arrows) to navigate your submarine
3. Collect sea creatures by touching them or moving near them
4. Avoid obstacles like anchors, rocks, and seaweed
5. Watch your oxygen level - don't run out!

## Sea Creatures to Discover

Find and collect various sea creatures:
- 🐠 Tropical Fish (10 points)
- 🐟 Fish (10 points)
- 🐡 Pufferfish (20 points)
- 🦈 Shark (30 points)
- 🐙 Octopus (25 points)
- 🦑 Squid (25 points)
- 🐢 Sea Turtle (30 points)
- 🦀 Crab (15 points)
- 🦞 Lobster (20 points)
- 🐚 Shell (15 points)
- ⭐ Starfish (35 points)

## Dangers

Avoid these obstacles that drain your oxygen:
- ⚓ Anchors
- 🪨 Rocks
- 🌿 Seaweed

## Explorer Ranks

Reach different depths to earn ranks:
- **Novice Diver** 🔰: 0-99 points
- **Brave Explorer** 🎖️: 100-249 points
- **Skilled Navigator** ⭐: 250-499 points
- **Expert Diver** 🏅: 500-999 points
- **Master Explorer** 🌟: 1000+ points

## Tips

- Keep moving to explore more areas
- Collect rare creatures for more points
- Avoid obstacles to preserve oxygen
- Try to discover all creature types!
