# Alphabet Soup

A delightful alphabet learning game where kids find letters floating in a magical soup bowl! Letters float and bob around like they're in a real soup, making learning the alphabet fun and engaging.

## How to Play

1. Click "Start Game" to begin
2. Look at the target letter displayed at the top
3. Find and tap that letter in the soup bowl below
4. Letters float around like they're in real soup!
5. Progress through the entire alphabet from A to Z
6. Try to complete all 26 letters as fast as you can!

## Features

- **Interactive Letter Soup**: Letters float and bob in a soup bowl with realistic animations
- **Progressive Learning**: Find letters in alphabetical order (A, B, C... Z)
- **Visual Feedback**:
  - Correct letters bounce and disappear with celebration
  - Wrong letters shake to provide gentle correction
  - Target letter pulses to keep focus
- **Timer**: Track how fast you can complete the alphabet
- **Touch-Friendly**: Large, easy-to-tap letter tiles (60x60px minimum)
- **Mobile-Optimized**: Responsive design works perfectly on phones and tablets
- **Beautiful Design**: Purple gradient theme with smooth animations

## Educational Benefits

- **Alphabet Recognition**: Helps children recognize all 26 letters
- **Sequential Learning**: Reinforces alphabetical order
- **Visual Discrimination**: Practice identifying specific letters among many
- **Fine Motor Skills**: Develops touch accuracy and hand-eye coordination
- **Focus and Concentration**: Finding specific letters requires attention to detail

## Gameplay Tips

- Look carefully for the target letter among all the floating letters
- The target letter appears multiple times in the soup - find any of them!
- Letters move slowly - take your time to find the right one
- Try to beat your previous time on the next playthrough!
