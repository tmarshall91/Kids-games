# Resource Empire

A simple economy management game where you gather resources, build structures, and grow your empire!

## How to Play

1. **Gather Resources**: Start with basic resources (wood, stone, food, gold)
2. **Build Structures**: Construct buildings to produce more resources
3. **Manage Economy**: Balance production and consumption
4. **Grow Population**: Build houses to increase your population
5. **Advance Days**: Progress through days to collect production

## Game Resources

- **🪵 Wood**: Used for construction, produced by Lumber Mills
- **🪨 Stone**: Used for construction, produced by Quarries
- **🌾 Food**: Consumed by population, produced by Farms
- **💰 Gold**: Premium currency, produced by Gold Mines

## Buildings

### Production Buildings

- **🏭 Lumber Mill**: Produces 5 wood per day
  - Cost: 20 wood, 10 stone, 30 gold

- **⛏️ Quarry**: Produces 4 stone per day
  - Cost: 15 wood, 15 stone, 40 gold

- **🚜 Farm**: Produces 10 food per day
  - Cost: 25 wood, 20 gold

- **⚒️ Gold Mine**: Produces 3 gold per day
  - Cost: 30 wood, 40 stone, 50 gold

### Special Buildings

- **🏠 House**: Increases population by 5
  - Cost: 40 wood, 30 stone, 20 gold

- **🏪 Market**: Boosts all production by 10%
  - Cost: 50 wood, 50 stone, 100 gold
  - Can build up to 3 markets

## Features

- **Resource Management**: Balance production and consumption
- **Building System**: 6 different building types
- **Population Growth**: Expand your settlement
- **Production Bonuses**: Markets boost all production
- **Manual Harvesting**: Get immediate resources when needed
- **Auto-Save**: Progress saved automatically
- **Achievements**: Unlock achievements for milestones
- **Progressive Difficulty**: Food consumption scales with population

## Game Mechanics

### Daily Cycle
- Click "Next Day" to advance time
- All buildings produce their resources
- Population consumes food (0.5 food per person per day)
- Check your production rates to plan ahead

### Food Management
- Population needs food each day
- If food runs out, you'll get a warning
- Build more farms to feed growing population
- Manual harvest provides emergency food

### Building Strategy
- Start with Lumber Mills for wood production
- Build Farms early to feed your population
- Quarries provide stone for larger buildings
- Gold Mines are expensive but provide valuable gold
- Markets multiply your production

## Achievements

- **First Builder**: Build your first structure
- **Survivor**: Reach day 10
- **Veteran**: Reach day 50
- **Metropolis**: Reach 100 population
- **Gold Rush**: Accumulate 1000 gold

## Tips for Success

1. **Balance Resources**: Don't focus on just one resource
2. **Feed Your People**: Always maintain food production
3. **Build Markets**: They boost ALL production
4. **Plan Ahead**: Check production rates before building
5. **Manual Harvest**: Use it when you're short on resources
6. **Grow Slowly**: Stable growth is better than rapid expansion

## Controls

- **Build**: Tap/click the "Build" button on any building card
- **Next Day**: Advance time and collect resources
- **Manual Harvest**: Get immediate resources without waiting
