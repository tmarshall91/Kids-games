# Traffic Controller 🚦

A fun traffic management game where kids control traffic lights to keep cars moving safely through an intersection!

## How to Play

1. Tap **Start Game** to begin
2. Cars will approach the intersection from all four directions (North, South, East, West)
3. Tap on any traffic light to toggle it between RED and GREEN
4. Keep traffic flowing smoothly by managing the lights
5. Prevent cars from crashing in the intersection!

## Rules

- Cars will stop at RED lights before entering the intersection
- Cars will go through on GREEN lights
- Each car that safely passes earns you **10 points**
- If cars crash, you get a strike
- **Game Over** after 3 crashes!

## Tips

- Watch for cars approaching from all directions
- Don't let too many cars through at once
- Make sure the intersection is clear before changing lights to green
- Keep one direction green long enough for waiting cars to clear

## Features

- Touch-optimized controls for mobile play
- Colorful car emojis (🚗 🚙 🚕 🚐)
- Real-time traffic management
- Score tracking and statistics
