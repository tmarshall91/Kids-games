# Monster Truck Jumps 🚚

An action-packed jumping game where kids perform awesome stunts with a monster truck!

## How to Play

1. Tap **Start Game** to begin your adventure
2. Obstacles will scroll toward your truck from the right
3. Tap the **JUMP!** button to make your truck jump over obstacles
4. Time your jumps perfectly to avoid crashing!
5. Jump over ramps for extra points and perfect stunts

## Scoring System

Different jump types earn different points:

- **Perfect Jump** (⭐ over ramps): **100 points**
- **Good Jump** (👍 well-timed): **50 points**
- **Regular Jump** (🚀 safe landing): **20 points**

## Obstacles

Watch out for various obstacles:
- 🪨 Rocks
- 🌵 Cacti
- 🏔️ Mountains
- 🛢️ Barrels

## Tips

- Time your jumps carefully!
- Jump over ramps for maximum points
- The game speeds up as you progress
- Practice makes perfect!

## Features

- Dynamic obstacle generation
- Multiple stunt types with different scores
- Increasing difficulty
- Touch-optimized controls
- Fun emojis and animations
- Track your best stunts!

## Controls

- **Mobile**: Tap the JUMP button
- **Desktop**: Press SPACEBAR or click JUMP button
