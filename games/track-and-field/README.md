# 🏟️ Track & Field Events

A fun and engaging multi-event athletics game where kids can compete in three exciting track and field events: 100m Sprint, Long Jump, and Javelin Throw!

## 🎯 Game Overview

Test your athletic skills across three different events, each with unique mechanics and challenges. Earn medals based on your performance and compete to become the ultimate track and field champion!

## 🎮 How to Play

### Event Selection
- Choose any of the three events from the main menu
- Events can be completed in any order
- Each event can only be completed once per game
- Completed events show your medal on the event card

### Event 1: 🏃 100m Sprint
**Objective**: Run 100 meters as fast as possible!

**Controls**:
- Tap the yellow button rapidly to run faster
- The more you tap, the faster your runner moves
- Race against two AI opponents

**Scoring**:
- Faster times earn higher scores
- Aim to finish in under 15 seconds for maximum points
- Track your distance, time, and tap count

**Tips**:
- Tap as quickly as possible for best results
- Maintain a steady rhythm for consistent speed
- Watch the AI runners to gauge your performance

### Event 2: 🤸 Long Jump
**Objective**: Jump as far as possible!

**Controls**:
- Tap the green button to start your run-up
- Your runner automatically builds speed
- Timing is key - the button press starts the approach
- The jumper automatically jumps at the end of the runway

**Scoring**:
- Distance is measured in meters (4m - 8.5m range)
- You get 3 attempts - your best jump counts
- Maximum speed gives maximum distance

**Tips**:
- Let your runner build full speed
- The speed meter shows your current velocity
- Longer approach = better jump
- Each attempt gives you another chance to beat your record

### Event 3: 🎯 Javelin Throw
**Objective**: Throw the javelin as far as possible!

**Controls**:
- Use the slider to set your throwing angle (20° - 70°)
- Hold the throw button to build power
- Release to launch the javelin
- Power builds up to 100% over 2 seconds

**Scoring**:
- Distance is measured in meters (0m - 90m range)
- Angle and power both affect distance
- You get 3 attempts - your best throw counts
- Optimal angle is around 45°

**Tips**:
- 45° angle gives the best results
- Build maximum power for longer throws
- Watch the power meter fill up
- Release at 100% for maximum distance
- Experiment with different angles

## 🏅 Medal System

Performance in each event earns medals:

- 🥇 **Gold Medal**: 85+ points - Outstanding performance!
- 🥈 **Silver Medal**: 70-84 points - Great job!
- 🥉 **Bronze Medal**: 50-69 points - Good effort!
- 🏅 **Participation**: Below 50 points - Keep training!

## 📊 Scoring

- Each event is scored out of 100 points
- Total score is the sum of all three events (max 300 points)
- Score is based on performance metrics:
  - **Sprint**: Based on completion time
  - **Long Jump**: Based on jump distance
  - **Javelin**: Based on throw distance

## 🏆 Final Results

After completing all three events:
- View your medal collection (gold, silver, bronze)
- See your total points across all events
- Receive a final performance message
- Option to play again and improve your scores
