# Swimming Race 🏊

A fun and exciting swimming race game for kids! Tap rapidly to swim faster and race against the CPU to reach the finish line first.

## 🎮 How to Play

1. **Choose Your Distance**: Select either 50m or 100m race
2. **Start the Race**: Tap the "Start Race" button to begin
3. **Swim Fast**: Tap the red area rapidly to make your swimmer go faster
4. **Beat the CPU**: Race against the computer opponent
5. **Win Medals**: Complete the race quickly to earn gold, silver, or bronze medals!

## 🏊 Game Features

### Swimming Mechanics
- **Tap to Swim**: Each tap gives you a speed boost
- **Momentum System**: Your swimmer maintains velocity between taps
- **Realistic Physics**: Friction gradually slows you down if you stop tapping
- **Visual Feedback**: Water splash effects 🌊 appear as you swim

### Race Modes
- **50m Sprint**: Quick race for fast reflexes (15s for gold)
- **100m Distance**: Longer race testing endurance (30s for gold)

### Competitive Elements
- **CPU Opponent**: Race against a computer swimmer
- **Real-time Progress**: See both swimmers move across the pool
- **Progress Bar**: Track your completion percentage
- **Live Stats**: Monitor distance, time, and speed

### Performance Tracking
- **Distance**: Current position in meters
- **Time**: Race duration in seconds
- **Speed**: Current swimming velocity
- **Tap Count**: Total taps during the race

### Medal System
- 🥇 **Gold Medal**: Beat the target time
- 🥈 **Silver Medal**: Good performance
- 🥉 **Bronze Medal**: Complete the race
- 🏊 **Participation**: Keep trying!

**50m Target Times:**
- Gold: 15 seconds or less
- Silver: 20 seconds or less
- Bronze: 25 seconds or less

**100m Target Times:**
- Gold: 30 seconds or less
- Silver: 40 seconds or less
- Bronze: 50 seconds or less

## 🔧 Customization

Want to modify the game? Here are some ideas:
- Adjust `CONFIG.tapPower` to change tap strength
- Modify `CONFIG.cpuSpeed` to make CPU easier/harder
- Change `TARGET_TIMES` to adjust medal requirements
- Update colors in `style.css` for different pool themes

## 🆘 Troubleshooting

**Taps not registering?**
- Make sure you're tapping inside the red tap area
- Try refreshing the page

**Game running slow?**
- Close other apps/tabs
- Check your internet connection
- Try a different browser

**Can't beat the CPU?**
- Practice your tapping rhythm
- Try the 50m race first to build confidence
- Focus on consistent tapping rather than fast bursts
