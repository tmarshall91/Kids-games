# Baseball Batting ⚾

A timing-based baseball game where players must swing at the perfect moment to hit home runs!

## How to Play

1. Tap "Start Game" to begin
2. Watch the pitcher throw the ball
3. Tap "SWING!" when the ball reaches the strike zone
4. Perfect timing = Home Run! Good timing = Hit!
5. Try to hit all 10 pitches!

## Scoring

- **Home Run** (Perfect timing): 10 points
- **Hit** (Good timing): 5 points
- **Miss** (Poor timing): 0 points

## Controls

- **SWING Button**: Tap to swing the bat
