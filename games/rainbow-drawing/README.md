# Rainbow Drawing 🌈

Draw with magical rainbow trails that follow your finger!

## How to Play

1. **Choose a Style**: Select from rainbow, fire, ocean, sunset, or neon
2. **Pick Trail Size**: Choose thin, medium, or thick
3. **Draw**: Move your finger to create colorful trails
4. **Watch the Magic**: See the colors flow and glow!

## Features

- **5 Magical Styles**:
  - 🌈 Rainbow: Classic rainbow colors
  - 🔥 Fire: Hot yellows, oranges, and reds
  - 🌊 Ocean: Cool blues and purples
  - 🌅 Sunset: Warm golds and pinks
  - ✨ Neon: Bright fluorescent colors
- **3 Trail Sizes**: Thin, medium, and thick
- **Glow Effects**: Beautiful glowing trails
- **Dark Canvas**: Colors pop against black background
- **Smooth Drawing**: Responsive trail rendering

## Technical Details

- HTML5 Canvas with advanced effects
- Shadow/glow rendering
- Color cycling algorithms
- Touch-optimized for smooth trails
- Multiple color palettes

## Controls

- **Drag**: Draw with rainbow trails
- **Style Selector**: Change color palette
- **Size Buttons**: Adjust trail thickness
- **Clear**: Start fresh
