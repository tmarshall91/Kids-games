# Dance Move Matcher 💃

Match the dance moves as they appear! Test your reflexes and coordination in this fun rhythm game.

## How to Play

1. Tap **Start Dancing** to begin
2. Watch for the **dance move** shown on screen
3. **Quickly tap** the matching direction button (or use arrow keys)
4. Keep matching moves to build your **combo**!
5. The game speeds up as you progress - stay sharp!

## Controls

### Touch/Mouse Controls
- **Tap**: Press the direction buttons (↑ ↓ ← →)

### Keyboard Controls
- **Arrow Keys**: Use keyboard arrows to match moves faster
  - ↑ = Jump Up
  - ↓ = Squat Down
  - ← = Step Left
  - → = Step Right

## Objective

- Match as many dance moves as possible
- Build combos for bonus points
- Each correct move: 10 points + combo bonus
- Don't miss any moves or press wrong directions!

## Features

- **4 Dance Moves**: Up, Down, Left, Right
- **Combo System**: Chain correct moves for bonus points
- **Progressive Speed**: Game gets faster as you succeed
- **Visual & Audio Feedback**: See and hear your performance
- **Keyboard Support**: Play with arrow keys
- **Score Tracking**: Track your best combo

## Dance Moves

1. **Jump Up** (↑) - 👆
2. **Squat Down** (↓) - 👇
3. **Step Left** (←) - 👈
4. **Step Right** (→) - 👉

## Scoring System

- **Base Points**: 10 points per correct move
- **Combo Bonus**: +5 points per combo level
  - 1st move: 10 points
  - 2nd move: 15 points (10 + 5 combo)
  - 3rd move: 20 points (10 + 10 combo)
  - And so on...

## Game Over Conditions

- **Missing a move**: Failing to respond in time
- **Wrong direction**: Tapping the wrong button
- Combo resets to 0 when you make a mistake
