# 🦁 Zoo Keeper

Manage your zoo and keep all the animals happy by feeding them their favorite foods!

## How to Play

1. **Watch for Hungry Animals**: Animals with a ❗ symbol are hungry
2. **Select the Right Food**: Choose from meat 🍖, vegetables 🥬, fish 🐟, or fruit 🍌
3. **Feed the Animals**: Tap hungry animals to feed them
4. **Match Correctly**: Each animal has a favorite food preference
5. **Score Points**: +10 for correct food, -5 for wrong food
6. **Beat the Clock**: Feed as many animals as possible in 60 seconds!

## Features

- **12 Different Animals**: Diverse zoo population
- **4 Food Types**: Match the right food to each animal
- **Timed Challenge**: 60 seconds of intense zookeeping
- **Score Tracking**: Points for correct feeding, penalties for mistakes
- **Progressive Difficulty**: Animals get hungry at increasing rates
- **Visual Feedback**: Animations for correct/incorrect feeding

## Game Mechanics

- Animals randomly become hungry every 2 seconds
- Hungry animals show a ❗ indicator and shake
- Feed with correct food: +10 points, animal becomes happy
- Feed with wrong food: -5 points, animal stays hungry
- Time pressure increases challenge

## Scoring System

- **Correct Food**: +10 points
- **Wrong Food**: -5 points
- **Fed Count**: Tracks successfully fed animals
