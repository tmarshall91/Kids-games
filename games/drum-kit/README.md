# Drum Kit 🥁

Play a virtual drum kit! Tap drums, cymbals, and percussion instruments to create your own beats.

## How to Play

1. **Tap any drum** to play its sound
2. **Use keyboard keys** shown on each drum for faster playing
3. **Record your beats** with the recording feature
4. **Play back** your recorded rhythms
5. **Create music** by combining different drum sounds!

## Controls

### Touch/Mouse Controls
- **Tap/Click**: Play any drum or cymbal

### Keyboard Controls
- **Q**: Crash Cymbal (left)
- **P**: Ride Cymbal (right)
- **W**: High Tom
- **E**: Mid Tom
- **R**: Low Tom
- **A**: Hi-Hat
- **S**: Snare Drum
- **Space**: Kick Drum

### Recording Controls
- **Start Recording**: Begin recording your performance
- **Stop Recording**: Finish recording
- **Play Recording**: Play back what you recorded
- **Clear**: Delete the current recording

## Features

- **8 Different Drums**: Various percussion instruments
- **Realistic Sounds**: Web Audio API synthesized drum sounds
- **Recording Feature**: Record and playback your beats
- **Keyboard Support**: Play drums using keyboard keys
- **Visual Feedback**: Drums light up when played
- **Mobile-Optimized**: Touch-friendly drum pads

## Drum Types

1. **Crash Cymbal** - Bright, loud metallic sound
2. **Ride Cymbal** - Higher pitched cymbal
3. **Hi-Hat** - Quick, crisp cymbal sound
4. **High Tom** - High-pitched drum
5. **Mid Tom** - Medium-pitched drum
6. **Low Tom** - Deep, low-pitched drum
7. **Snare Drum** - Sharp, crisp drum sound
8. **Kick Drum** - Deep bass drum

## Fun Patterns to Try

**Basic Rock Beat:**
- Kick → Snare → Kick → Snare (with Hi-Hat throughout)

**Simple Fill:**
- High Tom → Mid Tom → Low Tom → Snare

**Cymbal Crashes:**
- Use crash cymbals at the start or end of patterns
