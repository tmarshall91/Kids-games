# Pet Race Game 🏁

Choose your favorite pet and race against other animals to see who's the fastest!

## How to Play

1. **Choose your racer** - Tap on the pet you want to race with
2. Tap **Start Race!** to begin
3. Watch the exciting countdown: 3... 2... 1... GO!
4. Cheer as your pet races against 3 opponents
5. See who crosses the finish line first!

## Features

- **6 different pets** to choose from: Dog, Cat, Rabbit, Turtle, Horse, Kangaroo
- **Automatic racing** - just sit back and watch
- **Random outcomes** - different winner each time
- **Exciting countdown** before each race
- **Visual effects** highlighting your chosen pet
- **Winner celebration** with results
- Race as many times as you want!

## Game Mechanics

- Each race has 4 competitors (your pet + 3 random opponents)
- All pets have randomized speeds each race
- Lanes are shuffled randomly
- Fair competition - anyone can win!
- Your pet is highlighted with a special glow
