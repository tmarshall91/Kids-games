# Zoo Feeding Time 🦁🐘

Help feed the hungry zoo animals by giving each one their favorite food!

## How to Play

1. Tap **Start Feeding** to begin
2. An animal will appear asking for food
3. Read what the animal wants
4. Tap the correct food from the options
5. Feed 5 animals to complete each level!

## Features

- **8 different zoo animals**: Lion, Elephant, Monkey, Panda, Giraffe, Bear, Zebra, Kangaroo
- **Learn food preferences**: Each animal has their favorite food
- **Multiple levels** with increasing variety
- **Instant feedback** showing if your answer is correct
- **Second chances** - try again if you pick the wrong food
- Score tracking across levels
- Cute animations and sound effects (text)

## Animals & Their Foods

- 🦁 Lion → 🥩 Meat
- 🐘 Elephant → 🥜 Peanuts
- 🐵 Monkey → 🍌 Bananas
- 🐼 Panda → 🎋 Bamboo
- 🦒 Giraffe → 🍃 Leaves
- 🐻 Bear → 🍯 Honey
- 🦓 Zebra → 🌾 Grass
- 🦘 Kangaroo → 🥕 Carrots

## Game Mechanics

- 5 animals per level
- 6 food choices (including the correct one)
- Wrong answers give you another chance
- Correct answers increase your score
- Progress through unlimited levels
