# 🧩 Tangrams

A classic tangram puzzle game for kids! Arrange colorful shapes to match the target picture.

## How to Play

1. **Tap Start** to begin the game
2. **Look at the target shape** shown at the top (gray silhouette)
3. **Drag pieces** to move them around
4. **Tap pieces** to select them
5. **Use Rotate button** to rotate the selected piece
6. **Match the target** - when all pieces are in the right position, you win!

## Controls

### Desktop
- **Click and Drag**: Move pieces around
- **Click Piece**: Select a piece
- **🔄 Rotate Button**: Rotate selected piece 90 degrees
- **↺ Reset**: Start the puzzle over

### Mobile
- **Tap and Drag**: Move pieces around
- **Tap Piece**: Select a piece
- **🔄 Rotate Button**: Rotate selected piece 90 degrees
- **↺ Reset**: Start the puzzle over

## How Tangrams Work

Tangrams are ancient Chinese puzzles where you arrange geometric pieces to form a specific shape. The challenge is to:

1. Figure out which piece goes where
2. Rotate pieces to the correct orientation
3. Position them precisely
4. Complete the target shape

## Completion System

- **Automatic Detection**: The game detects when pieces are close to their correct position
- **Snap Feature**: Pieces automatically snap into place when close enough
- **Visual Feedback**: Selected pieces are highlighted
- **Success Celebration**: Stars and congratulations when completed!

- **Geometry Basics**: Understanding shapes and rotation

## Tips for Success

- Start with the target silhouette and identify obvious pieces
- Look for corner pieces first
- Try rotating pieces if they don't fit
- Use the reset button if you get stuck
- Pieces snap into place when close to the right position
- Take your time - there's no timer!
