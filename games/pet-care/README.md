# Pet Care Game

A fun and interactive virtual pet game where kids can take care of their own digital pet by feeding, playing, and putting it to sleep!

## How to Play

1. **Meet Your Pet**: When you start the game, you'll meet your adorable virtual pet
2. **Watch the Stats**: Keep an eye on three important stats:
   - **Happiness** (😊): How happy your pet is
   - **Hunger** (🍎): How full your pet's tummy is
   - **Energy** (⚡): How energetic your pet feels

3. **Take Care of Your Pet**:
   - **Feed** (🍎): Tap the Feed button to give your pet food and increase hunger level
   - **Play** (🎾): Tap the Play button to have fun with your pet and increase happiness
   - **Sleep** (😴): Tap the Sleep button to let your pet rest and restore energy

4. **Interact**: Tap your pet directly to give them love and a small happiness boost!

## Game Features

- **Three Care Actions**: Feed, play, and sleep to keep your pet healthy and happy
- **Dynamic Pet Emotions**: Your pet's appearance changes based on their mood (happy, neutral, sad)
- **Real-time Stats**: Watch stats change in real-time with colorful progress bars
- **Visual Feedback**: Enjoy fun animations and particle effects for each action
- **Tip System**: Get helpful reminders when your pet needs attention
- **Interactive Pet**: Tap your pet for extra cuddles and love
- **Cooldown System**: Buttons have cooldowns to make the game more challenging

## Controls

### Touch/Click Controls:
- **Feed Button**: Give your pet food (+25 hunger, +5 happiness)
- **Play Button**: Play with your pet (+15 happiness, -10 energy)
- **Sleep Button**: Put your pet to sleep (+30 energy)
- **Tap Pet**: Give your pet attention (+2 happiness)

### Tips:
- Stats decrease over time, so check on your pet regularly!
- Don't let any stat get too low or your pet will become sad
- Playing costs energy, so make sure your pet is well-rested
- Each action has a cooldown period before you can use it again

### Game Mechanics:
- **Stat Decay Rates**:
  - Happiness: -0.5 per second
  - Hunger: -0.8 per second
  - Energy: -0.3 per second

- **Action Cooldowns**:
  - Feed: 2 seconds
  - Play: 3 seconds
  - Sleep: 5 seconds

- **Warning Thresholds**:
  - Low: 30%
  - Critical: 15%

## Educational Value

This game helps children learn:
- **Responsibility**: Taking care of a pet requires attention and consistency
- **Cause and Effect**: Understanding how actions affect the pet's wellbeing
- **Resource Management**: Balancing different needs (food, play, rest)
- **Time Management**: Checking in regularly to maintain pet health
- **Empathy**: Understanding and responding to the pet's needs
