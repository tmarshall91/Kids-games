# ⛳ Mini Golf

A fun and colorful mini golf game for kids featuring 6 creative themed holes with various obstacles, ramps, and challenges!

## 🎮 How to Play

1. **Aim Your Shot**: Drag the golf ball ⚪ in the opposite direction you want it to go
2. **Set Your Power**: The further you drag, the more powerful your shot
3. **Release to Putt**: Let go to hit the ball
4. **Reach the Hole**: Get the ball into the hole ⛳ to complete each course
5. **Complete All 6 Holes**: Try to finish the entire course with the lowest score!

## 🏌️ Game Features

### 6 Creative Themed Holes

1. **The Beginning** (Par 3) - A simple starter hole with one obstacle
2. **The Windmill ⚡** (Par 4) - Navigate through rotating windmill blades
3. **The Zigzag 〰️** (Par 4) - Maneuver through a zigzag pattern of walls
4. **The U-Turn 🔄** (Par 4) - Take a U-shaped path around obstacles
5. **The Maze 🌀** (Par 5) - Find your way through a challenging maze
6. **The Final Challenge 🏆** (Par 5) - Multiple obstacles for the ultimate test

### Scoring System

- **Eagle** 🦅: 2 strokes under par
- **Birdie** 🐦: 1 stroke under par
- **Par** ✅: Complete in par strokes
- **Bogey** 👍: 1 stroke over par
- **Double Bogey** 😅: 2 strokes over par

### Game Mechanics

- **Realistic Ball Physics**: The ball rolls with momentum and friction
- **Wall Bouncing**: Ball bounces off walls and obstacles
- **Obstacle Collision**: Rectangular and circular obstacles block your path
- **Power Meter**: Visual feedback showing your shot power
- **Stroke Counter**: Track your strokes for each hole
- **Total Score**: See your complete scorecard at the end

## 🎯 Controls

## 🚀 Getting Started

1. Open `index.html` in a web browser
2. Click "Start Game 🏌️" to begin
3. Drag the ball to take your first shot
4. Complete all 6 holes and check your final score!

## 🐛 Troubleshooting

- **Ball won't move**: Make sure you're dragging from the ball itself
- **Too much power**: Drag shorter distances for less power
- **Ball stuck**: Use the "Restart Hole" button to reset
- **Can't see full course**: Try landscape orientation on mobile
