# Animal Sounds

Learn what sounds different animals make! Tap animals to hear their sounds and test your knowledge.

## How to Play

**Explore Mode:**
1. Tap "Start!" to begin
2. Tap any animal to see/hear its sound
3. Learn all the different animal sounds

**Quiz Mode:**
1. Tap "Quiz Me!" to test your knowledge
2. Read the sound shown
3. Tap the animal that makes that sound
4. Get points for correct answers!

## Features

- 12 different animals
- Emoji-based animal representations
- Explore mode for free learning
- Quiz mode to test knowledge
- Sound text displays (Moo, Woof, etc.)
- Score tracking
- Immediate feedback
